Physics in this lab

  • Magic hierarchy: θ*n,k,m = 2cos(πk/(n+1)) · (α₁/αm) · θ*2,1. The 3D stack, telemetry and tab 3 use the exact SVD generalisation σk(γ, δ) of the chain coupling.
  • BM numerics (tab 4): plane-wave diagonalisation of the continuum Hamiltonian. Sector mode is exact for U = 0; U ≠ 0 triggers the full 2n·NG stack. The α-scan locates velocity zeros numerically — compare them with your candidate formula.
  • Inhomogeneous SC (tab 2): σθ(r) creates AA-island puddles coupled as a Josephson network; TBKT, Js, Pauli-limit violation and the competing IVC magnetic order follow MATTG phenomenology.
  • Visual conventions: gold = AA flat-band peak, cyan = SC condensate / weak link, rose = IVC spins & Dirac cone. The 3D lattice twist is magnified (tab 5) — the 2D map "true θ" mode shows the physical pattern.
